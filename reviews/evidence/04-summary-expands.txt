    view/runs-with-proofs holds 4 <details> rollups.

    A LIMIT OF THIS HARNESS, stated before the measurement rather than
    after it. jsdom does not implement the user-agent rule that hides a
    closed <details>'s content, so this probe cannot show you the
    collapsed rendering — `open` is read and set, and the text is read out
    of the DOM either way. What it therefore measures is the question doc
    06 §8.4 actually asks — whether the three checks and their inputs are
    REACHABLE from the rollup — and not the separate question of whether
    they are visually hidden until it is opened. That second question
    needs a browser and is recorded as not determinable here.

── FINDING: the badge-alone route the claim does not close ───────────────────

    `VerificationBadge` is exported from the package index. A table author
    who imports it directly gets a green rollup with nothing behind it —
    anti-pattern 4 exactly — and the compiler does not object:

    import { VerificationBadge } from "../../src/components/verification";
    
    export const attempt = <VerificationBadge verdict="verified" />;

    RESULT: ACCEPTED by the compiler
