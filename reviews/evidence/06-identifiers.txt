── what this probe cannot determine ──────────────────────────────────────────

    Whether the rendered glyphs FIT the space they are given is a layout
    question. jsdom computes no layout — every box is zero by zero — so
    this probe cannot say whether an untruncated identifier overflows its
    column or wraps. That is a browser measurement and belongs to #57.
    What is measured here is the content of the truncation, which is what
    doc 06 §8.6 forbids losing.
