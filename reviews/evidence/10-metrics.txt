── FINDING, for a human ruling: one card is a monotonic lifetime count ───────

    Commits attributed renders a number that only ever goes up,
    with no window stated anywhere a reader can reach — not in the
    meaning line, not on hover, not to assistive technology. Measured:

    Commits attributed 4,181 Commits the ledger holds a commit_recorded event for. A record, not a verification.

    doc 06 §8.10 names this shape as its first example of the defect:
    "cumulative counts with no window". doc 06 §3.1 asks for the metric
    by name — "Metric cards: active agents, runs today, commits
    attributed, verification pass rate" — and states no window for it.
    The two sentences are in tension, and this review does not resolve
    a specification against itself.

    What is measured, and is not a matter of reading:
        - the number is a lifetime total (4,181 with the fixture's data);
        - the card states WHAT it counts and disclaims what it is not
          ("A record, not a verification"), so it is not a metric with
          no statement of meaning;
        - it states no PERIOD, and the adjacent "Runs today" card shows
          the same view already knows how to render one;
        - it is neutral grey, not a colour that claims anything.

    Recorded as a finding rather than a clean pass. A human doing sign-
    off should either rule that §3.1's naming of the metric settles it,
    or file the window as work.

── OBSERVATION: two different reasons share one sentence ─────────────────────

    Measured above, in the last two states: a rate held from a CACHE and a
    rate whose LIVE ATTEMPT ERRORED render the identical sentence —

        "The rate in hand was retained from an earlier check rather than
         measured now, so it is not shown as a current rate."

    The second is not a retained rate. It is a live measurement whose
    attempt failed, and doc 06 §6.1 asks copy to "say what was checked and
    what happened". The VERDICT is right in both — "Not measured", amber,
    no number — so this is not anti-pattern 1 and not anti-pattern 2: it is
    a reason given inaccurately, one level below the verdict. Recorded as
    an observation for the human, not as one of the ten.

    Note also what the 100% state does, since ADR-0038 left it open:
    neutral grey, not green, not amber. That is the "narrower reading"
    ADR-0038's last consequence called the safer default, and it is the one
    this build shipped.

    The real formatters, over numbers that invite abbreviation:

        formatCount(        0) = "0"

        formatCount(     1000) = "1,000"
        formatCount(     1234) = "1,234"

        formatCount(  1000000) = "1,000,000"
        formatCount(     4181) = "4,181"
        formatCount( 12345678) = "12,345,678"

        formatRate(90, 100) = "90%"
        formatRate(999, 1000) = "99.9%"
        formatRate(2, 3) = "66.6%"
        formatRate(1, 3) = "33.3%"
        formatRate(0, 0) = ""
        formatRate(4180, 4181) = "99.9%"

    No abbreviation (no 1.2M, no 1k), and the rate that is not quite 100%
    does not become 100%.

── what this probe cannot determine ──────────────────────────────────────────

    Whether a metric was CHOSEN to flatter is a judgement about what is
    absent, and no probe can enumerate the numbers a designer did not
    render. What is measured here is the two failures doc 06 §8.10 names
    by example — a cumulative count with no window, and a hidden failing
    pass rate — plus the rounding §6.2 forbids. The judgement itself is
    recorded in the review document, with the one live conflict this
    view carries stated there rather than resolved here.
